# AI and Machine Learning and Statistics

Duration: 4 months / 16 teaching weeks.
Schedule: 6 instructor-led hours weekly (96 total), plus 4 to 6 independent study hours.

Apply statistical reasoning and machine learning to a defined business problem. Students build baselines, prevent data leakage, evaluate models, communicate uncertainty, and examine responsible use. Deep learning and generative AI are introductory extensions.

## Prerequisites

Python and pandas proficiency; algebra; basic SQL recommended.

## Tools

Python; JupyterLab; NumPy; pandas; SciPy; scikit-learn; matplotlib; optional TensorFlow or PyTorch; Git.

## Learning outcomes

- Use descriptive statistics, probability, inference, and regression with appropriate assumptions.
- Prepare data and create leakage-safe training and validation pipelines.
- Compare supervised and unsupervised methods with suitable evaluation metrics.
- Communicate model limitations, fairness concerns, deployment needs, and monitoring plans.

## Weekly schedule

### Week 1 — AI and statistical reasoning

AI, ML and generative AI distinctions; problem framing; sample and population.

Practical: Define a prediction target, stakeholders and measurable success criteria.

### Week 2 — Descriptive statistics

Center, spread, distributions, missingness and exploratory plots.

Practical: Profile a dataset and identify quality and sampling concerns.

### Week 3 — Probability and sampling

Conditional probability, Bayes intuition, sampling bias, random variables.

Practical: Simulate sampling variability and interpret probability statements.

### Week 4 — Statistical inference

Confidence intervals, hypothesis tests, effect sizes and assumptions.

Practical: Analyze an experiment and distinguish significance from practical value.

### Week 5 — Regression foundations

Linear regression, residuals, assumptions, regularization concepts.

Practical: Fit and diagnose a regression baseline.

### Week 6 — Data preparation

Splits, leakage, imputation, encoding, scaling and feature engineering.

Practical: Build a preprocessing pipeline fitted only on training data.

### Week 7 — Classification

Logistic regression, confusion matrices, precision, recall, ROC and PR.

Practical: Evaluate a classifier under imbalanced outcomes.

### Week 8 — Midpoint practical

Statistics, preprocessing, baseline modeling and validation.

Practical: Deliver a defensible baseline with leakage checks.

### Week 9 — Tree models

Decision trees, random forests, boosting, complexity and overfitting.

Practical: Compare models using the same validation protocol.

### Week 10 — Model selection

Cross-validation, hyperparameter search, learning curves, nested concepts.

Practical: Tune a model while preserving an untouched final test set.

### Week 11 — Unsupervised learning

Clustering, scaling, PCA, interpretation and limits.

Practical: Create customer segments and assess their usefulness.

### Week 12 — Neural networks

Network structure, loss, optimization, regularization and training curves.

Practical: Train a small network and compare it with simpler baselines.

### Week 13 — Generative AI foundations

Tokens, embeddings, prompting, retrieval concepts, factuality evaluation.

Practical: Build a small retrieval demo and test unsupported answers.

### Week 14 — Responsible deployment

Explainability, subgroup checks, privacy, model cards, drift and monitoring.

Practical: Create a model card and serve a simple prediction interface.

### Week 15 — Capstone build

Error analysis, thresholds, business costs and reproducibility.

Practical: Finalize the pipeline and document decision tradeoffs.

### Week 16 — Capstone presentation

Statistical interpretation, model demonstration and limitations.

Practical: Defend evaluation choices and present a monitoring plan.

## Capstone

Customer churn prediction and decision support

Submit a reproducible notebook and pipeline, a baseline comparison, validation results, threshold analysis, model card, deployment demonstration, and business recommendations.

## Assessment

- Weekly practical work: 25%
- Knowledge checks: 10%
- Midpoint practical: 20%
- Capstone artifact: 35%
- Presentation and defense: 10%
